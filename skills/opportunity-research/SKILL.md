# Opportunity Research Skill

Use this skill when discovering, comparing, or validating candidate revenue systems.

## Objective

Find lawful, automatable economic opportunities and reduce uncertainty fast enough to rank them rationally.

## Required method

1. Define the opportunity mechanism in one sentence: who or what pays, for what, and why the value exists.
2. Prefer primary sources for protocols, APIs, pricing, technical rules, and market structure.
3. Identify the measurable source of edge or customer value.
4. Separate structural edge from temporary price discrepancy or hype.
5. Quantify capital requirements, operating costs, switching costs, dependency risk, and likely competition.
6. Identify what can invalidate the thesis.
7. Design the cheapest falsification experiment.
8. Record evidence and reversal conditions.

## Opportunity score dimensions

Evaluate at least:
- automation potential;
- time to first valid evidence;
- capital efficiency;
- recurring vs one-time revenue potential;
- gross and net margin potential;
- competition intensity;
- legal/compliance friction;
- dependency/platform concentration;
- technical complexity;
- observability;
- reversibility;
- ability to scale without proportionally scaling labor.

## Market/DEX research

For decentralized market opportunities additionally evaluate:
- chain/protocol mechanics;
- liquidity depth;
- fees;
- gas/priority costs;
- price impact;
- transaction ordering/MEV exposure;
- finality and revert behavior;
- oracle dependence;
- contract upgrade/admin risk;
- bridge/cross-chain assumptions;
- RPC/provider reliability;
- realistic execution latency.

Do not call a visible spread an arbitrage profit until executable net economics are modeled.

## Product/API research

For software/API opportunities additionally evaluate:
- evidence of developer/business demand;
- incumbent alternatives;
- buyer switching cost;
- measurable willingness to pay;
- support burden;
- abuse/fraud exposure;
- provider and data-source terms;
- marginal infrastructure cost;
- billing and collection friction;
- retention potential.

## Output

Every research task should produce:
- thesis;
- supporting evidence;
- contradictory evidence;
- assumptions;
- economics;
- confidence;
- falsification test;
- recommendation: investigate / prototype / defer / reject;
- reconsideration conditions if rejected.
