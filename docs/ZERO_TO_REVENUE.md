# Zero-to-Revenue Graduation Model

> **Current ownership note:** the filename is historical. Under the Epinnox architecture, this document is a **ZLJ evidence and intelligence qualification ladder**, not a grant for ZLJ to take capital into production.

ZLJ starts with no assumed edge. Market hypotheses, data products, features, and models must earn trust through evidence.

> **ZLJ sees. Benjamin decides. Watchman governs. The Hand executes. The Book remembers and proves.**

## Stage 0 — Opportunity / hypothesis discovery

Goal: create a ranked map of market questions and potentially useful predictive mechanisms.

Required outputs may include:

- market mechanism or opportunity thesis;
- instrument and horizon;
- required observations/features;
- dependency map;
- likely costs and execution frictions relevant to the hypothesis;
- automation potential;
- measurable falsification test.

No live financial exposure.

## Stage 1 — Research prototype

Goal: prove the phenomenon can be observed and modeled.

Required outputs:

- working data ingestion or observation mechanism;
- normalized/provenance-bearing data;
- feature definitions;
- explicit timing assumptions;
- known limitations;
- first falsification results.

## Stage 2 — Historical replay / deterministic simulation

Goal: evaluate the hypothesis under historical or replayed conditions with leakage controls.

For short-horizon market work include realistic spread, fees, slippage assumptions, latency, price impact where material, failure/reject probability, data-timing limitations, and market-regime segmentation.

The result is evidence about a market/model hypothesis, not a Benjamin decision.

## Stage 3 — Live shadow observation

Goal: run against live external conditions without risking production capital.

Record what the model or ZLJ intelligence object **would predict** before outcomes are known. Retrospective cherry-picking is invalid.

Measure:

- predicted vs observed outcome;
- calibration;
- false positives/negatives;
- latency;
- data-quality failures;
- error rates;
- model competence by instrument/horizon/regime;
- stability and drift over time;
- estimated execution feasibility as an evidence input.

## Stage 4 — ZLJ production-intelligence readiness

Required:

- sufficient shadow/out-of-sample evidence for the capability's cadence;
- provenance and timing integrity;
- deterministic data-quality checks;
- model/version reproducibility;
- calibration evidence;
- drift/out-of-distribution handling;
- observability and alerting;
- rollback/quarantine path;
- security review;
- typed output contract for Benjamin.

The Root Agent may recommend promotion of an intelligence capability. Promotion does not authorize capital.

## Stage 5 — Benjamin shadow consumption

Once the bridge exists, Benjamin may consume the qualified ZLJ object in shadow decision cycles.

ZLJ continues to measure its own prediction quality. Benjamin separately measures decision quality.

## Stage 6 — Governed Epinnox use

Any real capital use occurs outside ZLJ:

`ZLJ intelligence -> Benjamin decision -> Watchman authorization -> The Hand execution -> The Book outcome lineage`

ZLJ does not graduate itself into a live execution system.

## Stage 7 — Scale the intelligence capability

Scale ZLJ throughput, coverage, model capacity, or instrument coverage only where marginal information value remains positive and qualification remains intact.

Re-evaluate latency, data quality, calibration, provider concentration, distribution shift, infrastructure cost, and operational risk.

## Automatic demotion

Any ZLJ intelligence capability may be moved backward to limited, shadow, research, quarantine, or rejected state when:

- predictive edge materially degrades;
- calibration error exceeds tolerance;
- data-quality controls fail;
- latency invalidates the intended horizon;
- external protocols/providers change;
- security assumptions change;
- distribution shift exceeds qualification;
- a superior model or data source makes the capability obsolete.

## Rejected hypothesis record

Every rejection should record:

- why it failed;
- evidence supporting rejection;
- conditions under which it may be reconsidered.

Example:

```yaml
status: rejected
reason: predicted micro-edge disappears after realistic spread and latency assumptions
reconsider_if:
  - market structure changes materially
  - data latency improves materially
  - a new model demonstrates out-of-sample improvement
```

## Invariant

**ZLJ capabilities graduate into better perception, not into independent capital authority.**
